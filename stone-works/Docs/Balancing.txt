STONEWORKS — BALANCING
=======================

Purpose
-------
This document defines concrete numerical production, resource-conversion and scale values for StoneWorks.

These values are balancing rules rather than technical implementation details. Gameplay design belongs in GameDesignDocument.txt; system ownership and implementation belong in TechnicalArchitecture.txt.


1. MINING YIELD
---------------

Destroying 1 mine tile produces:
- 1 Stone

Therefore:
- 10 destroyed mine tiles = 10 Stones
- 100 destroyed mine tiles = 100 Stones


2. STONE GRINDING
-----------------

Grinding 1 Stone in the processing machine always produces:
- 2 Sand

In addition, the Stone has a chance to produce 1 Gem of a specific color.

Gem probability per color:
- Red: 2%
- Orange: 2%
- Yellow: 2%
- Green: 2%
- Blue: 2%
- Purple: 2%

Total Gem chance per Stone:
- 12%

If no Gem is produced:
- 88% chance to produce a third Sand

Therefore, every Stone produces exactly 2 Sand, plus one of:
- 1 Gem of one of the six colors
- 1 additional Sand

Average output per Stone:
- 2.00 guaranteed Sand
- 0.88 additional Sand
- 0.12 Gems
- 2.88 Sand expected on average


3. RESOURCE SIZE
----------------

Sand is considerably smaller than Stone in physical representation.

This size difference should be visually obvious and should allow substantially more Sand to accumulate in the Sand collection area than Stones could occupy in the same physical space.

The exact physical dimensions remain an implementation/balance parameter and are not fixed by this document.


4. SAND COLLECTOR
-----------------

The Sand collector converts accumulated Sand into Sand Tower progress.

Conversion rate:
- 1,000 Sand = 1 Tower Pixel

Therefore:
- 10,000 Sand = 10 Tower Pixels
- 100,000 Sand = 100 Tower Pixels
- 1,000,000 Sand = 1,000 Tower Pixels


5. SAND TOWER SCALE
-------------------

Tower width:
- 100 pixels

Tower height:
- 1 pixel = 1 meter of tower height

Therefore:
- 100 pixels high = 100 meters
- 1,000 pixels high = 1,000 meters

The Tower is persistent long-term visual progression and continues growing as Sand is contributed.


6. BASELINE PRODUCTION RELATIONSHIPS
-------------------------------------

For N destroyed mine tiles:
- N Stones are produced.
- 2N Sand is guaranteed after grinding all Stones.
- 0.88N additional Sand is expected.
- 0.12N Gems are expected.

Expected total Sand:
- 2.88N

Expected Tower Pixels:
- 2.88N / 1,000

Expected Tower height in meters:
- 2.88N / 1,000 meters

Examples:

100 destroyed tiles:
- 100 Stones
- 288 expected Sand
- 12 expected Gems
- 0.288 expected Tower Pixels

1,000 destroyed tiles:
- 1,000 Stones
- 2,880 expected Sand
- 120 expected Gems
- 2.88 expected Tower Pixels

10,000 destroyed tiles:
- 10,000 Stones
- 28,800 expected Sand
- 1,200 expected Gems
- 28.8 expected Tower Pixels


7. BALANCING STATUS
-------------------

Confirmed baseline values:
- 1 destroyed mine tile → 1 Stone
- 1 Stone → 2 guaranteed Sand
- Each of 6 Gem colors → 2% chance per Stone
- Total Gem chance → 12% per Stone
- No-Gem result → 88% chance for a third Sand
- 1,000 Sand → 1 Tower Pixel
- Tower width → 100 pixels
- 1 Tower Pixel → 1 meter of height

These values should be treated as the current balancing baseline and changed here when balancing decisions change.
